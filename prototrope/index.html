The Bible had it first

Long before modern storytelling gave these ideas names, the Bible was already telling them: the underdog who defeats the giant, the prodigal who comes home, the reluctant hero called out of hiding, the sacrifice that saves everyone else. Prototrope traces the stories and tropes we recognize in books, movies and games back to where they first appeared.
