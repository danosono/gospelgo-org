You Matter. You are loved. Help Is Available Right Now.

If you are thinking about suicide, feeling overwhelmed, or afraid of what you might do, please reach out now. You are not a burden. Your life matters.

If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number now (911 in the U.S. and Canada).

Immediate Suicide Help

U.S. and Canada: Call or text 988 to reach the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline 24/7.

Crisis Text Line (supported regions): Text HOME to 741741.

Outside the U.S.: Contact local emergency services or find a country-specific hotline through Befrienders Worldwide.

If You Can, Do These 3 Steps

1. Tell one safe person that you need help right now.

2. Move to a safer place where you are not alone.

3. Remove or step away from anything you could use to harm yourself.

Call out to Jesus Christ

Jesus sees you and loves you. Call out to Him. He can do anything. The links below may help you connect with Him. You are not alone. He is with you and can give you strength, hope, and a way forward.

For Friends and Family

If someone you love is talking about suicide, take it seriously. Stay with them, help them call emergency services or a crisis line, and do not leave them alone if there is immediate risk.

You can be the bridge between them and life-saving care.
