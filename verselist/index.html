Verse List

View, create and share Bible verse lists.

Create a list of Bible verses by topic and share, or view a list shared with you. Visit Verse List

Verse Lists are deep linkable — found a list that speaks to you? Copy the link and share it directly.


Every verse, close at hand

Verse List makes it quick and easy to view, create or share a list of verses by any topic. Whether you're studying, teaching or just found something interesting, view it fast and share it with a friend. Great for Bible study - exploring themes, topics, typology, etc.

Developers: Verse List is also useful for developers who want to view a list before adding it to a json or other file. Verse List allows comma separated, quote wrapped verses for easy import. In fact, Verse List was created to facilitate GospelGo's development workflow. Example: "Matthew 28:6", "Mark 16:6", "Luke 24:6-7", "John 20:16-17", "Acts 2:24", "Psalms 16:10"
