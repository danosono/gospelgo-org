Trafficked? You Deserve Safety and Help.

If you are being controlled, threatened, forced to work, or forced into sex acts, what is happening to you is WRONG. Help is available.

If you are in immediate danger, call emergency services now (911 in the U.S.).

Immediate Trafficking Help

U.S. National Human Trafficking Hotline: 1-888-373-7888

Text in the U.S.: Text HELP or INFO to 233733 (BEFREE).

Website: U.S. National Human Trafficking Hotline.

Additional information: OUR Rescue Survivor Care, Destiny Rescue, Safe House Project Referral., Kaleido.

If You Can, Take These Steps

1. If possible, go where other people are present.

2. Contact emergency services or a hotline from a safer location.

3. Keep only safe information and avoid actions that increase risk.

Call out to Jesus Christ

Jesus sees you and loves you. Call out to Him. He can do anything. The video playlist linked below may help you connect with Him. You are not alone. He is with you and can give you strength, hope, and a way forward.

For Concerned Friends

If you suspect someone is being trafficked, contact emergency services or a trafficking hotline (on this page).

Focus on immediate safety and connecting the person with trained professionals.
