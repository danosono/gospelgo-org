Bible Peruser

Sections of text are highlighted by outline, theme, label or other categorization, chapter by chapter. The outline was parsed from the Berean Standard Bible dataset. Custom labels and highlights are simply insights during daily Bible reading and not intended to be theological statements. Starting with Matthew, the chapter content will be added during daily bible study by the Gospelgo developer.
